### Game description (Markdown)

```markdown
*The Magisterium burned. You are what walked out of the ash.*

**Hermetic Rifts** is a dark-alchemy action roguelite. You pilot a Vessel through the sealed chambers of a failed Great Work: aim every rite, slip through the swarm, and stand in the Transmutation Circle when the seal finally shows itself.

Time does not merely fill the floor with bodies. The chamber hardens. Elites hunt. A Guardian wakes to keep the Rift shut. Names you have not earned still print as *???*.

## About this demo

- **You aim.** Move with WASD, fire along the cursor, dash through the blow. Standing still is how a Vessel dies.
- **Hold the seal.** When the Transmutation Circle opens, channel it while the horde converges. Then you fight the Seal Guardian alone.
- **Two Vessels.** The Homunculus is clay and quicksilver, already waiting. A second name stays *??? · SEALED* until you earn it.
- **Hermetic Tablets.** Graft them into your frame as you level. The kind ones help. The rare ones cut both ways.
- **Three chambers.** Scorched Cinder Yard. Frozen Rime Dock. The Glacial Tomb below.
- **The Archives.** A codex of what you have killed, bound, and not yet recovered.

A full descent is a handful of minutes, right in the browser.

If a working goes well, or if it shatters, paste the run summary in the comments. The Archives are listening.

**Age suitability:** 13+

**AI disclosure:** Development used AI-assisted coding tools. Pixel art and audio are original authored assets.
```

Confirm the AI line before paste if the asset pipeline has changed.

### Game instructions

```
WASD / Arrows — Move
Mouse + Left Click — Aim and fire
Right Click — Secondary rite
Space / Shift — Dash
Q — Ultimate
1 / 2 / 3 — Pick a tablet on level-up
P / Esc — Pause (use P in fullscreen)
C — Copy run summary on the recap screen

Goal: survive the chamber, hold the Transmutation Circle when it opens, then defeat the Seal Guardian. A Vessel that stands still dies. Keep moving, aim every rite, and graft Hermetic Tablets as you level.

Win: complete the descent through the unsealed chambers.
Lose: the Vessel shatters when hit points reach zero.
```
