# Anti-idle bible

VS idle is not "too much power". It is **difficulty-as-quantity**. AOE always beats quantity, so a good combo plays the game.

RoR2 late game is **difficulty-as-quality**: elites, telegraphs, hold the point. Garlic cannot AFK a Malachite.

## Rules (do not ship a feature that breaks these)

1. **Standing still is punished.** Astral Watcher lasers, Noxious elite contact slow + corpse acid pools. Not extra HP sponges.
2. **At least one player verb every ~2 seconds.** Primary is **player-aimed** (locked A). Auto-fire is banned on primary. Dash/reload later.
3. **Teleporter.** Around minute 4 of a stage, hold a zone ~60s. The swarm comes *into* the zone. You cannot AFK outside it.
4. **Quality over quantity.** Soft cap ~80–120 living enemies. Late game is 6 elites, not 600 chickens.
5. **Items have hooks.** Example: `+40% dmg, −15% max HP`. `Dash kills, costs 1 HP`. No orbital garlic that solves the run.
6. **Obstacles are pressure, not cover.** Pillars break edge-kiting and eat bolts; they must not create AFK hide spots (bosses phase through, spitters still force movement). Hazards punish standing (slow + DPS) and reward timed Phase Step. Volatiles detonate only after an **aimed** Bolt and a 0.4s telegraph — not an AFK screen-clear.

Power fantasy stays: at minute 15 you **walk through** the horde. You do not sit and watch it die.
