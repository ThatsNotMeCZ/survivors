# Concept — Stage Director

Working title: **survivors**. Hybrid of Vampire Survivors **pacing** and Risk of Rain 2 **pressure**.

## Steal / leave

| | Take | Leave |
|---|---|---|
| Vampire Survivors | 15–20 min run, 1-of-3 draft every 30–45s, readable 32px sprites, fast snowball *feeling* | Full auto in all directions, screen-delete evolutions, empty infinite lawn |
| Risk of Rain 2 | Difficulty coefficient, monster director, elites, teleporter hold, movement as HP, items that change verbs | 3D, 100+ items, 20 survivors, co-op (v1) |

## Fantasy

You are not watching a build win. You **move through** a horde that gets *meaner*, not just denser. Late run = fewer bodies, scarier elites, a point you must hold.

## Combat

Mouse-aimed primary (locked **A**). WASD move. You win by kiting and hitting elites, not by standing in an orbital.

## v1 scope

- 2 survivors (4 skills each: primary, secondary, dash, ultimate)
- 12 items, all with a hook (no pure +damage win)
- 4 enemy archetypes + 3 elite modifiers
- 2 biomes, 3 stages + finale
- ~20 minute run
